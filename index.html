BookNest help centre

Answers to the questions members ask most.

Borrowing

How many books can I borrow at once?

Members can borrow up to five books at a time for 14 days each.

Can I renew a book?

Yes. Each book can be renewed twice, as long as nobody else has reserved it.

What happens if I return a book late?

A fine of ₹2 per day applies. You cannot borrow new books until it is paid.

Membership

How do I join the library?

Visit the front desk with a photo ID and fill in a short form. Your card is ready the same day.

Is there a student discount?

Students pay half the yearly fee when they show a valid college ID.
